 ## steps:1.	To develop a thorough understanding of sentiment analysis techniques by reviewing and critically evaluating existing literature on sentiment analysis methods
2.	To collect a diverse dataset of social media posts from Facebook and Twitter platforms and pre-process the data to remove noise, slang, and irregular text formats
3.	To design, develop, and implement machine learning models for sentiment analysis, considering the unique characteristics of social media language.
4.	Evaluate ML model performance by considering factors such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score.
5.	To investigate and propose solutions for challenges specific to sentiment analysis on social media, such as sarcasm detection, handling context-dependent language, and adapting to evolving sentiment expressions
6.	To demonstrate the practical applications of sentiment analysis in real-world scenarios, such as marketing, public opinion research, and social impact assessment
7.	To outline the limitations of the proposed sentiment analysis models and suggest avenues for future research.
Description:The research will focus on developing and implementing sentiment analysis techniques to automatically classify social media posts into different sentiment categories, such as positive, negative, or neutral. The model developed for sentiment analysis of social media (Facebook and Twitter) posts will leverage machine learning (ML) and natural language processing (NLP) methodologies. The use of these methodologies will provide valuable insights into the emotional tone of the conversations between different social media platform users.
